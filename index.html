Hills on the way

Height above sea level. The red line is you. Big climbs: Sojoryeong (≈km 238) and Ihwaryeong (≈km 249, highest point). Short steep hills after Hapcheon-Changnyeong Weir (≈km 478) and near Namji (≈km 513). Walking the bike up is normal.

Distances are approximate. The official length is about 633 km.

Emergency

🚑 119 Ambulance / Fire 🚓 112 Police 📞 1330 English help (24/7)

1330 is the Korea Travel Hotline. They speak English and can join a call with 119 to interpret. On a US phone dial +82-2-1330.

Before the trip (one time, on wifi)

  1. Open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it from the Home Screen once, wait for ✅ Offline ready at the top.
  2. Allow Location (Settings → Privacy → Location Services).
  3. Translate app: download Korean and English for offline use.
  4. Settings → General → Language & Region → Live Text on.
  5. Install Organic Maps and download the South Korea map (street map + hospitals, offline).
  6. Bring a power bank. GPS uses battery.
