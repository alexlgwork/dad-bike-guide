Route

Elevation

Big climbs: Sojoryeong (km 238) and Ihwaryeong (km 249, the highest point). Short steep hills near km 478 and km 513. Walking the bike up is normal.

Distances are approximate. The official length is about 633 km.

Help

Emergency
119 Ambulance / Fire
112 Police 1330 English

1330 is the 24/7 Korea Travel Hotline. They speak English and can join a call with 119 to interpret. From a US phone: +82-2-1330.

Offline downloads

Terrain map, landmark photos, real photos around every stop and top-pick hotel, and the sign reader. Do this once on wifi (about 65 MB, a few minutes). When every line shows a check mark, the badge on the map says Fully offline.

Settings
Before the trip (once, on wifi)
  1. Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it from the Home Screen and wait for Offline ready.
  2. Help → Download now (terrain, photos, sign reader).
  3. Allow Location when asked.
  4. Translate app: download Korean and English.
  5. Settings → General → Language & Region → Live Text on.
  6. Install Organic Maps and download the South Korea map.
  7. Bring a power bank.
